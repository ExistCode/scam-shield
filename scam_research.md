# Cek Dulu: Indonesian scam landscape, 2025–2026

Researched 1 Nov 2026 (WIB). All numbers come from IASC/OJK as quoted by the linked outlets. IASC counts are **cumulative since IASC launched (22 Nov 2024)**, not yearly. Example messages are illustrative, written by us from the described modus, and are not real quotes.

**QRIS sticker swap.** IASC doesn't rank this one separately, but BI and news coverage show it's active. Fraudsters stick their own QR over a merchant's or mosque donation box's QRIS. **Fake gold sites.** OJK flagged sites like "Antamlogamulia-id.com" imitating the official logammulia.com and asking for transfers to personal accounts ([Detik, 29 Jun 2026](https://finance.detik.com/moneter/d-8552960/ojk-bongkar-modus-tipu-tipu-nasabah-hingga-bobol-rekening)).

## 2. Headline numbers

- **702,342 reports** to IASC from 22 Nov 2024 to 30 Sep 2026. There were 1,372,483 reported accounts and 702,833 blocked. **Rp811.1 M** of funds were blocked and **Rp207.16 M** returned. 173,718 phone numbers were reported ([Kontan, 5 Oct 2026](https://keuangan.kontan.co.id/news/iasc-terima-702342-laporan-kasus-penipuan-hingga-september-2026)).
- **About 80% of victims report more than 12 hours after being scammed**, and funds hop through 4 layers of accounts within minutes ([Detik, 29 Jun 2026](https://finance.detik.com/moneter/d-8552960/ojk-bongkar-modus-tipu-tipu-nasabah-hingga-bobol-rekening)). This is the pitch hook: **speed matters**.
- **West Java has the most reports**: 131,445 of 637,055 nationally to Jul 2026 ([Kompas Bandung](https://bandung.kompas.com/read/2026/08/21/210922178/ojk-jabar-catat-131445-laporan-scam-tertinggi-di-indonesia)).
- **About Rp7 T in total losses reported**, per OJK in Oct 2025 ([Kompas, 21 Oct 2025](https://www.kompas.com/tren/read/2025/10/21/193000165/10-modus-scam-paling-marak-di-indonesia-menurut-ojk-apa-saja-)). Komdigi cites **Rp7.5 T**, sourced from the Global Anti-Scam Alliance ([Kompas, 2 Jul 2026](https://nasional.kompas.com/read/2026/07/02/20330831/komdigi-sebut-banyak-lansia-jadi-korban-scam-ai-yang-tiru-suara-pejabat)).
- **Per-report losses by modus** are available in the Kompas Dec 2025 article. For example, fake call averages Rp36.17 jt per report.

## 3. Elderly, parents and AI voice

- Komdigi's Deputy Minister Nezar Patria (Jul 2026) said many elderly people fall for AI scams that clone officials' or family members' voices. Scammers "type the text, then just play it back" ([Kompas, 2 Jul 2026](https://nasional.kompas.com/read/2026/07/02/20330831/komdigi-sebut-banyak-lansia-jadi-korban-scam-ai-yang-tiru-suara-pejabat)).
- OJK had received **1,379 reports of AI-indicated fraud** by Jun 2026, including voice and face cloning and "AI robot trading" ([CNBC Indonesia, 7 Sep 2026](https://www.cnbcindonesia.com/market/20260907170046-17-765940/ribuan-warga-ri-ketipu-ai)).
- Implication for Cek Dulu: **don't claim deepfake detection**. Instead, verify the request: urgency, a transfer to a personal account, an OTP request, or a "new number". Tell the user to hang up and call back on the old number.
- **Unconfirmed:** I found no official age breakdown of IASC victims. Don't cite a "% of victims are elderly" figure.

## 5. Recommended demo and test coverage

| Scam | Why | Cek Dulu feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Fake call / family impersonation** ("Ma, ganti nomor", fake BCA CS) | Biggest loss (Rp2.09 T), hits parents, ties into the AI voice trend | Voice note → ASR → verdict, plus call-back advice and **guardian alert** |
| **APK via WA** (wedding invite, courier) | Classic parent trap, and an instant **bahaya** verdict is easy to show | APK file detection + "already installed? turn off data, call 1500888" |
| **Belanja online / fake promo** | #1 by report count | Screenshot (vision) + account → cekrekening.id step |
| **QRIS swap** | Visual, Indonesia-specific, and your demo already works | QR decode, merchant name vs. claimed name |
| **Phishing link / hadiah palsu** (+ an investment pitch if time) | Common SMS/WA pattern | Link check (domain lookalike, e.g. antamlogamulia-id.com vs logammulia.com) |

Also add **aman** controls: a real J&T update, a family chat, a legitimate bank promo with no link or OTP request. These show the bot doesn't flag everything.

## Case study: fake police video call ("OTT kurir narkoba")

- **Official source:** Polda Metro Jaya (@poldametrojaya), Threads, 17 Oct 2025: [HATI-HATI! Video call polisi gadungan dengan modus OTT kurir narkoba](https://www.threads.com/share/BAaL3PI2tO/)
- **Modus operandi (per the post):** the scammer reaches the victim on a video call, stages a "police station" setting, and accuses the victim of being the recipient of a drug package.
- **Common red flags (agent rules):** real police don't interrogate over a WhatsApp video call; "don't hang up" or "don't tell your family" is pressure; PPATK/Kejaksaan never ask for transfers.
- **Bot's next steps:** hang up, call 110 or the nearest police station directly, tell your family, and don't transfer anything.
- **Pitch line:** "The other day I came across a reel about this scam, and Polda Metro Jaya had already posted an official warning about it."
- **Demo:** use a self-written script turned into a voice note (not a real recording).
